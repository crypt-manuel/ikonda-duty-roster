Filling in the roster

With the mouse

  • Pick a shift in the palette, then click or drag across cells to fill them.
  • Clicking a cell that already holds that shift clears it. Right-click erases any cell.
  • 🖍 Highlighter marks priority cells; ⇄ Swap exchanges two cells.
  • Click a day number in the header to mark or unmark a public holiday.

With the keyboard — quicker for long runs

Click any cell to place the cursor, then:

MDENOL
Paint that shift. The cursor steps right on its own, so a week of mornings is just M six times.
